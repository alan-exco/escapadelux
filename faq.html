FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Can’t find your answer? Email admin@escapadelux.com or call +267 72 829 770.

Where is Escapade Lux?

Our luxury Residence is located in 34268 Tshamawe, Block 8 just off Airport Rd, Block 8, Gaborone.

How much does a room cost?

Our suite rate depends on multiple factors including suite choice, number of people and availability and promotions and discounts being offered at the time of booking. Generally, our base rates range from P1,350 pn to P2,500 pn however we regularly run promotions and discounts for bookings made directly with us or on our website.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. Coffee, tea, juices, breads, pastries and full English breakfast are all available.

Breakfast serving is between 7am and 9:30am unless with prior arrangement.

Do you offer any special packages or discounts?

Yes, we offer seasonal packages and discounts for extended stays. Please check our website or contact us for more information.

Do you do group booking discounts?

We welcome groups and may offer discounts subject to the type of booking and any promotions at the time

Can I book all the rooms as a group booking and get a discount?

Yes, an all-suite group booking can be arranged. Group rate depends on number of nights stay, availability, season, promotions and discounts being offered at the time of booking

When I book do I have to pay a deposit?

All bookings require credit card details for assurance purposes. A 20% deposit of full cost is taken at the time of booking and credited when final payment is made. The deposit is forfeited if the booking is cancelled within 4 days prior to 2pm on the date of arrival.

We strongly advise all guests to read the terms and conditions and cancellation policy before confirming a booking.

What are the rooms like?

Suites vary in size and amenities. We have one deluxe ¾ twin room, one deluxe queen suite, two executive suites and a Presidential Suite with a larger ensuite bathroom and a lounge area with balcony.

  • All bedrooms are ensuite
  • All suites have a Smart TV with 54 DSTV Essentials channel package
  • Reliable Hi-speed Starlink WiFi network through the guesthouse
  • Mini fridges in all suites including bottled water
  • Coffee, tea and condiments in every room
  • Luxurious toiletries, towelling, robes and slippers in all rooms
  • Daily servicing

We will go out of our way to provide other services on request. (add on services may come at additional cost)

Are children welcome?

Of course! Infants (< 4 years old) stay free. All infants and children under 16 years old must stay in the same suite with at least one parent. We have a porta bed available for a child staying with a couple (additional cost)

What time is check in and check out?

Check in is 3pm. Check out is 11am

Do you serve lunch and dinner?

No. However we can provide guests with information of closest restaurants and take away delivery. We can also prepare group dinners and lunches on request. (additional cost)

What services and facilities do you provide?

The property facilities include 5 ensuite bedrooms and spacious living areas including a dining area, lounge area, media room, swimming pool, BBQ area. Our kitchen provides a choice of daily full breakfast or continental breakfast.

The property has on property vehicle parking and 24 hr security surveillance.

Starlink hi-speed internet is provided throughout and DSTV Essential is available in all suites, the lounge area and the media room.

Can I smoke on the property?

We are required to follow Botswana Department Of Tourism regulations which prohibits smoking and vaping on the property.

Is Escapade Lux close to shops and restaurants?

Yes, Airport Junction Shopping Mall and restaurants are a short walk or drive (5 mins).

Gaborone CBD is a short drive (15 mins).

What is the nearest airport?

The nearest airport is Sir Seretse Khama International Airport, which is approximately 7km from Escapade Lux.

Do you provide an airport shuttle service?

Not currently. However, we can arrange a shuttle or taxi for you on request.

How many people in rooms?

Two adults max. per suite and up to three people in some suites provided at least one person is an adult

Can I do my own cooking and laundry?

Unfortunately, Hospitality and Tourism and the Health and Safety regulations prohibit that. However, if you wish, we can arrange a chef to prepare lunch or dinner for your period of stay (group bookings only, additional cost).
