ABOUT US

Our story, from 2025.

All-inclusive luxury, elegance and service. Why stay anywhere else?

Built with intention.

The Guesthouse was built with luxurious intention and curated elegance in mind. Opening in mid 2025, we are developing a reputation for high-quality service and luxurious facilities, receiving many wonderful reviews from our local and international visitors. And we aim to keep improving!

Our suites and services

Five en-suite bedrooms with elegant features and fittings and luxurious amenities including robes, slippers, towelling and toiletries. Each suite also features a smart TV with DSTV, a workspace, hi-speed Starlink WiFi and a mini fridge with refreshments.

Our acclaimed five-star-rated continental and full English breakfast is loved by guests and included in your reservation.

Calmness and relaxation

The Guesthouse has a number of spacious public areas for calm relaxation and privacy, including a large open lounge, a media room and a large pool deck. There is also a fully equipped 10-seat boardroom if you need to entertain clients and colleagues for private and discreet business meetings.

Where we are

Located in Gaborone’s Block 8 along Airport Road, the Guesthouse is a short drive from Sir Seretse Khama International Airport and Airport Junction shopping mall. Gaborone CBD is a 15-minute drive away.
