Cancellation policy

All bookings made online require a deposit of 20% of the booking fee at the time of booking.

For bookings excluding peak periods

Unless booked as a non-refundable discounted reservation:

  • Cancellation more than 14 days prior to the first day of the booking: fully refunded (less any commission fees).
  • Cancellation 14 days or less from the first day of the booking: 50% of deposit forfeited.
  • Cancellation 48 to 96 hours from the first day of the booking: 100% of deposit forfeited.
  • Cancellation 0 to 48 hours, or no show: 100% of booking fee.

Peak periods (Christmas, New Year, Easter and national holidays)

To secure a reservation during peak periods a non-refundable 20% deposit is required.

Note: in most cases, if a new booking date is mutually agreed with you and management, penalties may be waived at the sole discretion of management.
